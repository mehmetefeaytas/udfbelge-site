FAQ

A UDF file won't open

Make sure the extension is .udf. Some tools such as WhatsApp Web rename UDF files to .zip. The app also tries to open .zip and .eyp packages, but renaming the file back to .udf is the most reliable fix.

The e-signature is not shown

The app detects a sign.sgn file and warns you before editing a signed document. Full certificate verification is on the roadmap. If you edit and save a signed document, the signature becomes invalid.

Purchases and restoring

Premium is offered as a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. To restore, open Premium → Restore Purchases. To cancel a subscription use Premium → Manage Subscription or your App Store account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

iCloud sync isn't working

Sync is included with Premium and requires an iCloud account on the device. Check the UDF Belge switch under Settings → Apple Account → iCloud.

Ads and privacy

The free tier shows ads. Your document content is never shared with ad networks. You can change ad choices under Ad Privacy Settings. Upgrade to Premium to remove ads entirely.

Where are my files stored?

Documents are kept in the app's local container and are never uploaded to a server. See the privacy policy for details.
