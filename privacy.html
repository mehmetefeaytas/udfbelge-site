Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

UDF Belge treats the confidentiality of legal documents as a core design principle. The app does not operate a server that receives your document content.

Document content

  • Documents stay on device. UDF files you open, edit or convert are stored only in the app's local container.
  • No uploads. PDF/DOCX/PNG/TIFF conversion, pagination and document analysis all run on your device's processor.
  • No sharing with ad networks. Document text, file names, folder names and UYAP verification codes are never passed to any advertising or analytics SDK.
  • On-device assistant. Summaries, party/date extraction and semantic search use Apple's on-device natural language framework; content never leaves the device.

iCloud sync

Optional and available to Premium users. When enabled, documents are written only to the private CloudKit database of your own iCloud account. The developer cannot access this data. Apple's terms apply.

Advertising

The free tier may show banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. A Premium subscription or the lifetime purchase removes all ads.

  • Google may process a device identifier and ad interaction data to serve ads.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland a consent form is shown before any ad request; no ads are requested without consent.
  • App Tracking Transparency is optional and requested only before the first full-screen ad. If declined, ads are non-personalised.
  • You can change your choices at any time under Ad Privacy Settings in the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple via StoreKit. Payment details never reach the developer. Entitlement is cached on device and remains valid for up to 7 days while offline. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Permissions

  • Photos: requested only when you choose to insert an image into a document.
  • Notifications: used only if you enable them.
  • Tracking (ATT): optional, used for ad personalisation only.

Data we collect

The developer does not collect personally identifying information, does not run its own analytics infrastructure and does not sell data. Apart from ad serving, no data leaves the device.

Storage and deletion

Documents remain on device until you delete them. Items in the trash may be permanently removed after 30 days. Deleting the app removes its local data; iCloud backup and sync behaviour is under your control.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date.
