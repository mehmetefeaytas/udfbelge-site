UDF Belge

Open, edit and export UYAP .udf documents on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Your documents never leave your device. The document text, file name and UYAP verification code are never uploaded to any server. All analysis runs on device.

What you can do

  • Open UDF files straight from Files, Mail or the share sheet.
  • Edit text, headings, tables and images with a block-based editor that preserves formatting.
  • Export to PDF, DOCX, PNG, TIFF and plain text; convert several documents at once.
  • Organise your archive with folders, tags, favourites, comments and a restorable trash.
  • Use the on-device assistant for summaries, parties, dates and verification codes.
  • Premium adds iCloud sync, watermark-free output and an ad-free experience.

Platforms

iOS 17+ (iPhone), iPadOS 17+ and macOS 14+. One purchase covers all your devices.

Independent app

UDF Belge is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Turkish Ministry of Justice, UYAP or any official institution. It only works with the .udf file format used by UYAP.
